# Safe claim to evidence map

This is a prospective map from Roadmap §§1.4–1.5 to the registered evidence
workflows. It records no experimental results. Claim wording stays bounded by
the roadmap; each family remains unavailable until its listed evidence and
gates are complete.

| Safe claim family | Required experiment evidence | Main evidence / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Component-specific necessity | Proposal-Assisted Opening Necessity; Single-Reproduction Necessity; Source-Artifact Exclusion Necessity; External Verification Necessity | Predeclared collapse families and collapse decision; no generic novelty inference |
| Primary source-excluded admission behavior | Data and Domain Evidence Validation; Protocol Invariant Validation; Baseline Implementation Validation; Primary Confirmatory Evaluation | Independent construction, verification, source-excluded synthesis, and fresh admission; primary metrics and paired statistical gates |
| Conditional Byzantine operating region | Compromised Reproducer Robustness; Compromised Verifier Robustness; Byzantine Bound Violation | Locked `f_R=1` / `f_V=1` operating region; above-bound evidence remains boundary/failure evidence |
| Safe dormancy under resource limits | Evidence Scarcity and Dormancy | Logical-cycle horizon, expiry, resumability, and safe dormancy verification |
| Epistemic and capability limits | Shared Epistemic Failure Boundary; Capability Under-Specification Boundary | Equal-transcript premise and predeclared capability contracts; no inference outside tested cases |
| Heterogeneity boundary | Heterogeneous Reproduction Boundary | Declared heterogeneity regimes, synthesis/admission outcomes, and boundary evidence |
| Admission timing and resource cost | Admission-Delay Decomposition; Efficiency Measurement | Typed phase timing and descriptive resource measurements; efficiency remains outside inferential superiority claims |
| Secondary transportability | Secondary-Dataset Generalization | CICIoT2023 target and pseudo-domain results; wording explicitly excludes claims of real administrative independence |

Roadmap §1.5 remains the exclusion list: no generic novelty, treating
reproducibility as proof of truth, unlimited liveness, security above the fixed bounds, Sybil,
privacy, deployment, or robustness claims outside the declared envelopes.
Publication tables and figures serialize measurements and evidence states;
they do not generate free-form claim prose. Project completeness verification
checks the planned cell inventory, terminal evidence, manifest dependencies,
comparison currency, the Byzantine region, and safe dormancy before returning a
complete report.
