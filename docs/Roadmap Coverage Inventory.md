# Roadmap Coverage Inventory

## 1. Authority

- **Roadmap:** `docs/FedSIRA_Roadmap.md`
- **Repository:** `naslouby-salahe/FedSIRA`

This inventory is derived only from the authoritative roadmap. It records implementation-bearing requirements, conditional requirements, scope/claim boundaries, negative requirements, assumptions, and non-implementation obligations without changing their meaning.

## 2. Inventory Rules

- Every roadmap obligation is represented explicitly and at the smallest useful independently verifiable contract level.
- Requirement IDs are stable. Once an ID is referenced by a GitHub issue it is never renumbered or reused.
- `MANDATORY_ACTIONABLE` and `CONDITIONAL_ACTIONABLE` requirements must map to implementation work and objective verification evidence.
- Scope restrictions, exclusions, assumptions, terminology, rationale, and claim boundaries that create no direct implementation task remain traceable as `NON_IMPLEMENTATION`.
- `UNMAPPED` means implementation-bearing work has not yet been linked to its final GitHub issue(s).
- `AMBIGUOUS` means the roadmap itself contains a material contradiction or unresolved decision that must not be guessed.
- The roadmap remains the scientific and implementation authority. This inventory may decompose and trace it but may not extend, weaken, reinterpret, or silently correct it.

## 3. Classification

### Coverage

- `MAPPED` — mapped to the owning milestone and one or more implementation issues.
- `UNMAPPED` — implementation-bearing requirement not yet linked to final implementation issue(s).
- `NON_IMPLEMENTATION` — traceable boundary/assumption/rationale with no direct implementation unit of its own.

### Readiness

- `READY` — the roadmap gives sufficient implementation detail.
- `AMBIGUOUS` — a material roadmap contradiction or missing decision requires a clarification issue.
- `BLOCKED` — implementation cannot proceed until an external prerequisite explicitly required by the roadmap becomes available.

### Requirement Types

`Algorithm`, `Architecture`, `Artifact`, `Assumption`, `Claim Boundary`, `CLI`, `Configuration`, `Dataset`, `Experiment`, `Failure Semantics`, `Metric`, `Negative Requirement`, `Preprocessing`, `Provenance`, `Reporting`, `Reproducibility`, `Scope`, `Statistics`, `Test`, `Threat Model`.

## 7. Audit Invariants

The inventory has been independently reread and attacked from the following ten perspectives before the planning PR was opened:

1. **Section-completeness audit** — fresh pass across the roadmap from preamble through §43, including the unnumbered Configuration YAML block.
2. **Atomicity audit** — requirements were split when implementation, verification, failure, or ownership could independently succeed or fail.
3. **Negative-constraint audit** — forbidden claims, prohibited fallbacks/substitutions, no-post-hoc rules, raw/test-role restrictions, and source-exclusion restrictions were re-derived independently.
4. **Scientific-semantics audit** — claim contract, state machine, proposal screen, reproduction objective, verification, Krum, final gate, attacks, baselines, metrics, and theory were checked against their exact roadmap meaning.
5. **Dataset/configuration audit** — N-BaIoT and CICIoT2023 identity, schemas, mappings, roles, caps, scaling, evidence minima, configuration ownership, deterministic hashes, and raw-data adaptation rules were rechecked.
6. **Experiment-matrix audit** — every §30 experiment and §31 count contract was re-derived independently, including pre-core/post-core dependencies and invalid/evidence-insufficient cell semantics.
7. **Statistics/claim audit** — inferential unit, pairing, exact tests, Holm families, bootstrap, materiality, collapse logic, comparison registry, and §35 claim-state machinery were rechecked.
8. **Artifact/provenance audit** — execution phases, retries/recovery, artifact lifecycle, DAG, fingerprints, selective invalidation, reuse, provenance, outputs/results separation, and idempotency were rechecked.
9. **Dependency/ordering/reporting audit** — §29 execution ordering, CLI ownership, required tables/figures, claim registry, project-completeness verification, and manuscript readiness were rechecked against upstream requirements.
10. **Fresh hostile re-derivation** — a final roadmap-first pass intentionally ignored this inventory's structure and searched for missing or contradictory obligations; it surfaced REQ-274 and REQ-332 as explicit ambiguities rather than silently resolving them.
